# Course 6: Sequence Models

Model ordered data with recurrent networks, embeddings, sequence-to-sequence methods, attention, and transformers.

## What you should be able to do

Choose representations and decoding methods appropriate to text, speech, and other sequences; understand how attention changes information flow.

## Learning path

### [Week 1: Recurrent models](week-1/week.md)

RNNs, backpropagation through time, language modeling, gradient problems, GRUs, LSTMs, bidirectionality, and depth establish sequence modeling.

### [Week 2: Word embeddings](week-2/week.md)

Distributed word representations, Word2Vec, negative sampling, GloVe, sentiment classification, and debiasing cover semantic features.

### [Week 3: Sequence-to-sequence tasks](week-3/week.md)

Beam search, BLEU, attention, speech recognition, and trigger-word detection introduce structured sequence outputs.

### [Week 4: Transformers](week-4/week.md)

Self-attention, multiple attention heads, and the transformer stack replace recurrence with context-sensitive interactions.
