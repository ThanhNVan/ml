# Course 5: Convolutional Neural Networks

Learn convolution operations, compare influential architectures, localize and segment objects, then study face recognition and style transfer.

## What you should be able to do

Reason about spatial dimensions, receptive fields, architecture tradeoffs, detection outputs, and feature-based similarity objectives.

## Learning path

### [Week 1: Convolution foundations](week-1/week.md)

Filters, padding, stride, channels, pooling, and complete CNN layers turn image grids into learned features.

### [Week 2: Architectures and reuse](week-2/week.md)

Classic networks, residual connections, 1×1 convolution, Inception, MobileNet, EfficientNet, transfer learning, and augmentation show ways to improve accuracy and efficiency.

### [Week 3: Detection and segmentation](week-3/week.md)

Localization, landmarks, sliding windows, bounding boxes, IoU, NMS, anchors, YOLO, U-Net, and transpose convolution address structured image outputs.

### [Week 4: Recognition and generation](week-4/week.md)

Siamese networks and triplet loss support one-shot face recognition; neural style transfer optimizes content and style objectives.
