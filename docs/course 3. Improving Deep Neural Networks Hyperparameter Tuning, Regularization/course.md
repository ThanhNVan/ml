# Course 3: Improving Deep Neural Networks: Hyperparameter Tuning, Regularization and Optimization

Diagnose generalization problems, improve optimization, and tune deep-network training systematically.

## What you should be able to do

Use train/dev/test evidence to choose between bias and variance remedies; train with modern optimizers and normalization; tune the most influential hyperparameters first.

## Learning path

### [Week 1: Practical setup](week-1/week.md)

Bias/variance analysis guides regularization, dropout, input normalization, initialization, and gradient checking.

### [Week 2: Optimization](week-2/week.md)

Mini-batches and exponentially weighted averages lead to momentum, RMSprop, Adam, learning-rate decay, and a more realistic view of local optima.

### [Week 3: Tuning and deployment behavior](week-3/week.md)

Hyperparameter search, batch normalization, softmax classification, and framework implementation complete the training workflow.
